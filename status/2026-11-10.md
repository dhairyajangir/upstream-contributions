# Upstream contribution checkpoint — 10 November 2026

This is a snapshot of the contribution campaign begun on 2 October 2026. Counts are as checked on 10 November; submissions are not counted as merges. The [evidence index](../evidence.json) links each campaign merge to its upstream PR and actual merge commit.

## Results

- **13 of 200** target upstream PRs have merged. One is in an official company repository, [Microsoft VS Code](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/339846), toward the separate target of 50. No OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Google DeepMind merge is included in that company count.
- The campaign has **37 tracked PRs**: 13 merged, 15 open and nine closed without merging. Of the 33 new submissions in that set, 13 merged, 13 are open and seven closed unmerged. No open campaign PR was a draft in this snapshot.
- The broader qualified external inventory has **60 PRs**: 18 merged, 16 open and 26 closed unmerged. Five of those merges predate this campaign and are excluded from the 13 above. The [case-study index](../README.md) covers the 13 campaign merges in 11 case studies. Personal-repository work, other authors' changes and issues do not count as upstream PR merges.
- These are **gross** merge counts. Full reversions, retained-work totals, all-in contributor hours and a complete count of unresolved requested changes have not been measured; they cannot be inferred from these numbers.

## What the completed sample shows

For the **13 merged campaign PRs**, the median time from PR creation to merge was **10.1 hours**. Eight had a formal review by a GitHub owner, member or collaborator before merge, with a median time to the first such review of **9.0 hours**. Ten had a qualifying collaborator top-level comment or formal review before merge, with a median first-response time of **6.6 hours**. These samples exclude unresolved and closed-unmerged work. They are small, omit some possible human reviewers and are affected by selection bias; they do not predict the wait for an open PR.

Of the 22 campaign PRs with a resolved outcome, 13 merged and nine closed unmerged. This describes resolved work, not a forecast acceptance rate: 15 PRs remain open. Closure reasons include simultaneous-PR limits, a duplicate, stale closures, project decisions not to accept a use case and a PR that missed repository submission requirements. For example, [Bootstrap #43061](https://github.com/twbs/bootstrap/pull/43061) was closed after feedback about its PR template and test placement; [Three.js #34747](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/pull/34747) was closed because the maintainer considered the reported use outside the intended usage. Revisions are not automatically resubmitted when maintainers have declined a change.

## Deadline scenarios

The campaign cutoff is **1 December 2026 at 23:59:59 IST**. At approximately 01:30 IST on 10 November, 187 more merges were needed in about 21.94 days: **8.52 per day**. The aggregate observed pace was about **0.333 merges per day**.

| Illustrative assumption through the cutoff | Approximate total campaign merges |
| --- | ---: |
| No additional merges | 13 |
| Continue the observed aggregate pace | 20 |
| Sustain twice the observed aggregate pace | 28 |

These are arithmetic scenarios, not probabilities or promises. They do not model maintainer capacity, review delays, future submissions or reversions. If all 15 currently open campaign PRs merged, the total would be 28; further independent accepted work would still be needed to reach the target.
