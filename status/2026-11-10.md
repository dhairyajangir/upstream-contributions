## What the completed sample shows

For the **13 merged campaign PRs**, the median time from PR creation to merge was **10.1 hours**. Eight had a formal review by a GitHub owner, member or collaborator before merge, with a median time to the first such review of **9.0 hours**. Ten had a qualifying collaborator top-level comment or formal review before merge, with a median first-response time of **6.6 hours**. These samples exclude unresolved and closed-unmerged work. They are small, omit some possible human reviewers and are affected by selection bias; they do not predict the wait for an open PR.

Of the 22 campaign PRs with a resolved outcome at 01:30, 13 merged and nine closed unmerged. This describes resolved work, not a forecast acceptance rate: 15 PRs were open in that snapshot. Closure reasons include simultaneous-PR limits, a duplicate, stale closures, project decisions not to accept a use case and a PR that missed repository submission requirements. For example, [Bootstrap #43061](https://github.com/twbs/bootstrap/pull/43061) was closed after feedback about its PR template and test placement; [Three.js #34747](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/pull/34747) was closed because the maintainer considered the reported use outside the intended usage. Revisions are not automatically resubmitted when maintainers have declined a change.
